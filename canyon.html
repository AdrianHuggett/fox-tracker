Plan

With Select, drag across the map to box several drawings, then drag any of them to move them all. Copy and Paste (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V) work in any phase, so you can carry a plan into the next one.

Your plan saves in this browser. To share it with your R4s, copy the plan code and paste it into their board.

Quick reference

Three alliances fight in the Lost Canyon, one per territory: Stormrage (left), Flameguard (right) and Iceguard (bottom). Every building pays points each minute you hold it: Bastion, Fortress and Frozen Citadel 1,800, Cluster of Ruins 600, Ruins 180, Stronghold or Airport 60. The three Fortresses open at 20:00 and the Frozen Citadel at 40:00, for the last 20 minutes. The squad that lands the final blow takes the building. Press Our territory to set where FOX starts. Map and building positions from the WoSTools Canyon Clash Planner.
