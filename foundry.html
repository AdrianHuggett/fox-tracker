Plan

With Select, drag across the map to box several drawings, then drag any of them to move them all. Copy and Paste (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V) work in any phase, so you can carry a plan into the next one.

Your plan saves in this browser. To share it with your R4s, copy the plan code and paste it into their board.

Quick reference

Buildings pay a one-time bonus on first capture, then points every minute you hold them. The Imperial Foundry, Munitions Warehouse and Mercenary Camp open at 15:00. Weapon Workshops appear late in the battle at random spots. Leaving to heal costs 12 minutes. Unlock times differ slightly between guides, so check the in-game panel. The map is the in-game Foundry layout (map image from the WoSTools Foundry Planner). FOX starts in the left safe zone by default; press Swap sides if you start on the right. Personal points are half the alliance points.
