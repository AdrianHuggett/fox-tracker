01 · Classification

Your event categories, checked

These labels organize the guide; they are not official in-game tabs. An event may have more than one label.

Recurring events

Alliance battles, progression contests, hero events, and repeatable mini-games. Some are solo even when FOX coordinates them.

Examples: Foundry Battle · Snowbusters · Icefire Warhymn League

Seasonal

Tied to a named holiday or season. Activities and rewards can change on each return.

Confirmed here: Halloween Party

Special / limited

One-time events, collaborations and limited releases without a confirmed annual schedule.

Spectral Ice Shack · Twin Star Adventure · Wander Theater

Deals

Optional purchases alongside an event. Mia's Fortune and Frosty Fortune also have free participation, so they appear under recurring events too.

Mia's Fortune · Frosty Fortune
02 · State progression

What changes by hero generation

The day counts are community estimates, not guaranteed unlock dates. Check the event calendar and the rules in your state.

Gen 1

about day 0

Natalia · Jeronimo · Molly · Zinman

Early alliance territory and first hero cycle.

  • Fortress battles arrive early; strongholds follow as the state develops.
  • Hall of Chiefs and Lucky Wheel are early hero routes. Wheel: Zinman.

Gen 2

about day 40

Flint · Philly · Alonso

Castle, pets, Fire Crystals, then first cross-state competition.

  • Sunfire Castle around day 53; pets around day 54; Fire Crystal Age around day 60.
  • SvS / King of Icefield eligibility around day 80, subject to cycle. Wheel: Flint.

Gen 3

about day 120

Logan · Mia · Greg

More advanced progression and event rewards.

  • Fortress reward pools can gain Fire Crystal and pet items.
  • Wild Brawl becomes possible in eligible states. Wheel: Mia.

Gen 4

about day 195

Ahmose · Reina · Lynn

Hero event transition.

  • Hero's Mission replaces Gina's Revenge around this stage.
  • War Academy is estimated around day 220. Wheel: Lynn.

Gen 5

about day 270

Hector · Norah · Gwen

New hero rewards and deeper Fire Crystal progression.

  • Hero's Mission shard target changes with state progress.
  • Wheel: Hector. Save gems before its rotation.

Gen 6

about day 360

Wu Ming · Renee · Wayne

Current-event rules still control availability.

  • Wheel: Renee; hero reward pools advance again.
  • Newer modes such as Winter Siege may roll out separately and are not guaranteed by Gen 6.

Generation dates and progression: community server timeline. Wheel heroes: Lucky Wheel guide.
